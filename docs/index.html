Safety Histogram Nextgen Demo

This branch replaces the legacy Webcharts renderer with a Chart.js-based implementation.

Open the interactive test page

Manual test checklist

  • Change Measure and confirm the distribution and axis labels update.
  • Apply Site/Sex/Race/Arm/Participant filters and confirm participant counts update.
  • Toggle Normal Range and confirm the gray range overlay appears.
  • Edit Lower/Upper limits and confirm the histogram redraws.
  • Change Algorithm, Quantity, and Width; confirm bin counts and labels update.
  • Switch X-axis Ticks between linear and bin boundaries.
  • Hover a bar and confirm tooltip/footnote describe the bin.
  • Click a bar and confirm the linked listing appears.
  • Use listing pagination, search, sortable columns, and CSV export.
  • Change Group by and confirm grouped histograms render.
Known gaps: p-value annotations are browser-side screening approximations pending reviewer acceptance or deferred statistical validation; automated visual regression and stronger accessibility evidence are still pending. Listing pagination/search/sort/export and the Chart.js normal-range overlay now have executable harness coverage in PR #1.
